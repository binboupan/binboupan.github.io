Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 6, 2026

Thank you for using Radiyo (“the App”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our internet radio player application.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or require any personal information to use the App.

There is no account registration, login, or user profile.

b. Automatically Collected Information

The App does not use analytics, tracking tools, or advertising identifiers.

However, basic technical information such as your IP address may be temporarily processed by your device or by radio stream providers as part of normal internet streaming. This information is not collected or stored by us.

c. Streaming Content

The App streams radio content from third-party radio stations. We do not record, log, or monitor what you listen to.

2. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not use your information for tracking, analytics, advertising, or profiling.

Any information processed is strictly limited to what is technically necessary for the App to function (such as connecting to radio streams).

3. Third-Party Services

The App connects to third-party radio stations to stream audio content. These radio stations may have their own privacy practices, which are outside our control.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the radio stations you choose to listen to.

4. Advertising

The App does not display advertisements and does not use ad networks.

5. Data Retention

We do not retain personal data because none is collected.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected, there is no risk of children’s data being stored.

7. Security

Because the App does not store personal data, the risk to your privacy is minimal. We still take reasonable steps to ensure the App functions securely.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored, there is no data to access, modify, or delete.

If you have questions or concerns, you may contact us at any time.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected by revising the “Last updated” date above.
